THRIVE Caffeine & sleep companion

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026 · Published by BrainCare Foundation

This policy describes how the THRIVE mobile app handles information. THRIVE is a caffeine and sleep tracker built for teens — the short version is that everything you log stays on your own device.

01What the app stores

If you use THRIVE, the app may store on your device: drinks you log, caffeine totals, an optional first name, bedtime and settings, sleep ratings, streaks, journal notes, and optional questionnaire answers.

02Where it is stored

All of this stays on your device. THRIVE does not create an account, does not have a THRIVE server, and does not upload this information to BrainCare Foundation.

03What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs in THRIVE. We do not share THRIVE data with third parties for advertising.

No account required No ads No analytics SDKs No data sale

04Who the app is for

THRIVE is intended for people 13 and older. It is a wellness tracker, not a medical device, and it does not provide medical advice.

05How to delete your data

You can erase everything at any time in Settings → “Erase all data on this phone”. Uninstalling the app also removes it. The app has a built-in drink database and makes no network requests of its own; the only links that open the internet are ones you tap (this policy, data sources, and email support).
